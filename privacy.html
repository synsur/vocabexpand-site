The app

What it collects

Nothing. The app has no account and no analytics, and it does no tracking. It has no networking code of its own.

It is designed for the App Store privacy label “Data Not Collected”. That the label holds is an inference so far, and is still to be checked.

The app is not released. This page describes it as it is built to work, and it will be updated when the app ships. Last changed 22 November 2026.

What it talks to

Apple, and nothing else. The app’s traffic is Apple’s: StoreKit for purchases, the App Store’s record of the date you first got the app, and Apple-hosted asset packs, which bring the words and their recordings. If a check of how you said a word is ever added in the form that uses them, Apple’s speech models would be Apple’s traffic too.

There is no server of ours. Apple’s rule for the framework that delivers the packs is that it may not be used “to identify a user or device”.

What stays on your iPhone

The app keeps these in files on your iPhone: your settings (the notification time, whether IPA is shown, sounds, and its own counters for offers and for asking for a rating); your join day; for each day, the step you reached, what you picked, and whether you opened and finished it; the latest day opened, the version of the word library it last used, and the words it has seen withdrawn; the notification requests it has handed over, delivered or withdrawn; for each word you keep, the day you kept it, your answers when it came back, when it is next due and whether it is rested; the words you chose not to keep; any sentence you write; and what the App Store last told it about your VocabExpand+. The app alone writes them, and it has no code to send them anywhere.

“Delete all my sentences” in Settings removes your sentences. A reinstall is expected to lose your kept words, your sentences and your progress with the app’s storage: that is an inference, and what a device backup restores has not been verified. There is no sync in version 1.

The widget reads one file the app writes for it: for each day, the word’s id, the word, its tiles, the question and the notification time; for a finished day, the respellings, the stressed tile and the hook; and the words back that day.

The microphone

The app asks for the microphone on your first tap of Record yourself, and shows that it is recording while it does.

Your take is a temporary file, deleted when you leave the screen. The app keeps no recording and sends nothing.

Saying no costs only the recording, and nothing paid needs the microphone.

Whether the app will ever check how you said a word is not decided. Both designs on file keep no recording and send nothing; one of them would also ask for permission to use speech recognition, on the phone.

Photos

The app asks only for permission to add to your photo library, for Save Image in the share sheet.

Notifications

Notifications are planned on the phone: the day’s word once a day, a notice the day after the phone’s last planned word, one reminder while a free trial is set to renew, and one 30 days before a yearly plan renews.

Purchases

VocabExpand+ is bought through the App Store, with StoreKit 2 on the device. No other purchase or analytics service is in the app, and nothing about you or a purchase leaves the phone through it. The app keeps on the phone what the App Store last told it about your VocabExpand+: whether it is active, whether a free trial is running and set to renew, and when it expires.

The day you joined

The app reads a signed date from the App Store: the date you first got the app. It decides which words you have been given, and it is why a reinstall keeps them. Getting that date may need a connection; until it arrives, the day of first launch stands in.

Age

VocabExpand is made for adults and teens. It is rated by its content and is never in the Kids Category.

The app carries Apple’s declared-age-range entitlement. What the app does with a declared range is decided before it ships, and this page will say so then.

Reporting a word

A wrong word is reported by email, with the word’s id. The email is yours to send: support@vocabexpand.com.

This website

  • This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • It has no analytics, no trackers, no forms and no embeds from other sites.
  • Every file it uses is served from the site itself: the pages, one stylesheet, one script, the fonts and the images. Nothing is loaded from a third party.
  • Its one script lights syllables and plays tile sounds when you tap a word. It makes no network request.
  • The typeface for words and IPA is served from this site and not from a font host.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. A server is sent the address a request comes from, and GitHub says that when a Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub.
