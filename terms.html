The app

VocabExpand is an iPhone app for iOS 26.0 and later. It gives one word per local date, the same word for everyone.

These are the product’s terms as they are decided. The app is licensed to you under Apple’s standard licence agreement for App Store apps, its Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

What is free

The day’s word and its whole loop; two returns for each word you keep, and Keep it again on a rested word for two more, as often as you like; every word you were given, and search over them; one word of your choice from before you joined; the say-it check if one ships; and the public page for each posted word.

What VocabExpand+ adds

Every word from before you joined, each with its whole loop, and search over every word from past days; up to two more returns for every word you keep, without asking, from 12 weeks after the second return and then from 9 months after the third, sooner after “Not yet” (a return that falls more than a week behind is skipped); and two more lock-screen looks, Word and Say. The day’s word is one word, the same for everyone, paying or not.

What it costs

$29.99 a year or $4.99 a month, each with a 7-day free trial, or $79.99 once.

VocabExpand+ is offered once at the end of the first day’s word, and when you open a word from before you joined, at most once a day and three times in all. Otherwise it waits in Today, Words, Search, the looks and Settings, none of which opens by itself, and a rested word’s page names it in one line. It is never offered inside a day’s loop, on a return, or as a notification.

Trials, renewal, and when a plan ends

Purchases are made through the App Store, with StoreKit 2 on the device. The App Store starts trying to renew a subscription in the 24 hours before it expires, so a trial that has not been cancelled may be charged from 24 hours before it ends. If notifications are on, one comes before a free trial ends while it is set to renew, and another 30 days before a yearly plan renews, which Today also shows.

When a paid plan ends, every word you opened while it was paid for stays open, kept or not, and every return granted while it was on stays, the longer ones included. The other words from before you joined lock, except the one you chose free, and so do the Word and Say looks. Words from before you joined that you open during a free trial stay open only once a plan is paid for: if the trial ends unpaid, they lock again and their returns stop showing, and a later purchase on the same iPhone brings them back as they were. A reinstall is expected to lose this record; that has not been verified.

The words you are given

Your join day is the App Store’s date for when you first got the app. The words given are the calendar’s days from the join day to today, opened or not, and a reinstall keeps them.

If a word is withdrawn

A false word is withdrawn by a reviewed pack version. Once it reaches the phone, at the app’s next run or background refresh the word leaves every screen. Until then its pending notifications and widget entries stay, and a phone that never downloads the pack keeps the library it shipped with.

The words themselves

A way to say a word, a meaning and an origin rest on four dictionaries (Cambridge, Longman, Dictionary.com and American Heritage). Wiktionary is quoted beside them, under its own label. The records are our own writing, and no definition text is copied.

Who it is for

Adults and teens. The app is rated by its content and is never in the Kids Category.
