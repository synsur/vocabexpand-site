Reach us

Write to support@vocabexpand.com.

In the app, “Something wrong with this word? Tell us”, on a word’s Keep step, opens your own mail with the word’s id. Settings also has a “Report a word” row.

Questions

Can I get it yet?
No. VocabExpand is not released, and no release date has been set.
What will it run on?
iPhone, with iOS 26.0 or later. It is built for iPhone only.
What does it cost?
It is free, with a paid tier, VocabExpand+: $29.99 a year or $4.99 a month, each with a 7-day free trial, or $79.99 once. Free, always: today’s word and its whole day, every return, every word you’ve been given.
Do I need an account?
No. VocabExpand has no account.
When does the word arrive?
The word’s notification comes once a day, at the time you choose: 9:00 unless you change it. The word itself opens at midnight where you are, and with notifications off it waits in the app.
Will I hear the word said?
In the app’s own audio, only from a recording of a person, played from its own button; a way with no recording is shown and labelled, not played. No synthetic voice says a day’s word there: that rule holds unless an automatic check of such a voice passes the bar set for it. VoiceOver, your own screen reader, does read the word. As of 21 November 2026, no word’s record holds a recording.
Does it tell me whether I said it right?
Not as it is designed today: you say the word aloud and, if you choose, record yourself and hear it back, with no verdict. Whether a check is added is not decided. If one is, it names the printed way it heard, and never gives a score or says “wrong”.
The way I say it isn’t listed.
A word’s page shows every way the four dictionaries print, and Wiktionary’s label for an English beyond the UK and the US. How anyone says a word is never called wrong. An English no source here records is not called wrong either, and not called the same as the UK’s: the record says nothing about it. If you think a record is missing something, tell us.
What happens to my recording?
It stays on your iPhone and is deleted when you leave the screen, or when VocabExpand next opens if it closed first. VocabExpand sends nothing you say.
When does a kept word come back?
Twice. The first return comes from two days after you keep it; the second from four weeks after you answer “Had it”, or from two days after “Not yet”. At most three words come back in a day, and a return never sends a notification.
Is there a streak?
No streak, no points, no count of days anywhere.
What if I delete the app?
The words you were given stay yours: your join day is the date the App Store has for your first download, and a reinstall keeps every word since. Your kept words, your sentences and your progress are stored with the app on the phone; a reinstall is expected to lose them, which has not been verified, nor what a device backup restores.
The microphone is off.
The app says: “The microphone is off for VocabExpand. You can turn it on in Settings.” Only recording yourself uses it; everything else works without it.
Where will I manage VocabExpand+?
The app’s Settings has a VocabExpand+ section with “Manage subscription” and “Restore purchases”.
What happens when VocabExpand+ ends?
Words from before you joined lock again, and the lock-screen look returns to the free one. A word from before you joined that you kept keeps its returns; its page locks.
A word looks wrong.
Tell us. Every claim in a record is held to the page it cites. A word found false is withdrawn: once the reviewed pack reaches your phone, the word leaves every screen at the app’s next run or background refresh.
Where do the words come from?
Every word is in at least one of four dictionaries, and its record rests on them, with Wiktionary beside them. The record page says how. The record
